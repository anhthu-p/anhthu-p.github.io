Background

My path here wound through three countries. I started with a BS in Pharmacological, Medical, and Agronomical Biotechnology at the University of Science and Technology of Hanoi, where I got hooked on bench science screening plant-derived compounds for anti-inflammatory activity. That pulled me to Carnegie Mellon for an MS in Biotechnology and Pharmaceutical Engineering, where I picked up the computational and engineering side of biology. Now I'm a PhD candidate in the Oncology program at the University of Pittsburgh, trying to stitch those two halves together to understand cancer cell behavior at a systems level. Lately I've also been pulling deep learning into the mix. I think it can surface patterns in biology that classical methods just can't see, so I've been studying the field (currently in a team of 4 building graph neural nets and differentiable diffusion models to predict spatial cell responses) and figuring out where it actually earns its place in my own research. It's still going, so stay tuned :D.

I also love baking and playing piano

Experience

University of Pittsburgh (May 2025 - Present)

Working with Dr. Wayne Stallaert and Dr. Steffi Oesterreich on the molecular drivers of quiescence in ER+ invasive lobular carcinoma. I deconvolve bulk RNA-seq from ILC tumors to isolate malignant-cell expression, nominate the FOXO3-p27 axis as a candidate driver of quiescence, and map single-cell cycle states across multiplexed breast tumor imaging.

Carnegie Mellon University (May 2023 - Jul 2023)

Research Associate with Dr. Huaiying Zhang, studying how overexpressed MAD1 promotes mitotic slippage in cancer cells. I purified and characterized the phase-separation behavior of a Mad1 coiled-coil truncation and automated condition-screening workflows on the Emerald Cloud Lab.

University of Science and Technology of Hanoi (Dec 2020 - Jul 2021)

Research Assistant with Dr. Hai Dang Nguyen, evaluating anti-inflammatory activity of plant-derived compounds from Amomum species. I helped identify Longifuran A, a novel benzofuran with low cytotoxicity and significant inhibition of IL-6, IL-1β, and TNF-α — work that contributed to two publications.
