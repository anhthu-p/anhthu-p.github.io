Tissue sections used for multiplex immunofluorescence (mIF) often suffer from physical artifacts — tears, folds, and loss — that corrupt downstream single-cell and spatial analyses if left undetected. This project builds an automated QC pipeline to flag tissue loss and folding across large imaging cohorts, replacing slow and inconsistent manual review.

Applied across 800 breast cancer tissue microarray (TMA) cores and HTAN multiplexed imaging data, the pipeline enabled reliable single-cell spatial analysis at scale as part of my PhD research into cell cycle states in breast cancer.

[View the code on GitHub](https://github.com/anhthu-p/mIF-Tissue-LostFold-Detection)
